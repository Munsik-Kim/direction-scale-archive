# Preserved supplements

PROOF_SUPPLEMENT_EN.tex and .md are the complete existing V2 English proof sources supplied with v0.5. TYPESET_PROOF_SUPPLEMENT_EN.tex is its previous typesetting variant. Their mathematical content is retained, including the historical checker coverage defect and its correction. Historical review wording refers to an AI review workflow; it is not a new human peer review or formal proof certificate.

Scope_Addendum_EN.tex preserves the v0.5 derivations. Editorial changes replace local font paths, private delivery prose, obsolete source locators and an unexecuted “next protocol” with archive status. No mathematical extension was completed.

Historical private source inventories, previous opinion files, large traces and original execution packets are not bundled. Public numeric sources are mapped by results/evidence_index.csv, and exclusions by docs/DATA_AND_LICENSES.md. These sources are preserved evidence, not a newly audited proof.

No supplement PDF is relabeled as a new compiled product. Typesetting commands are in ../build.sh and require an independently available compiler.
